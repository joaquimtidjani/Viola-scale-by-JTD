Fingerings

Only the fingerings printed by Carl Flesch are shown. Notes without a number are not fingered in the book.
Source: Carl Flesch, Das Skalensystem (violin). Contents of the book, for each key: exercises 1 to 4, the scale, arpeggios, broken thirds and chromatic scale on one string (1st, 2nd, 3rd octave); exercise 5, the same over three octaves (checked note by note against the book); then thirds, sixths, octaves, fingered octaves, tenths and harmonics (not included here). When Flesch prints two fingerings, the second one is below the staff.

Score

Violin fingerboard

Only the notes played are shown, on the string where they are played. The number is the finger printed by Flesch (“4/3”: same note taken by two fingers); empty circle: finger not marked. ½ = fingers close together (half step), T = fingers apart (whole step). The neck is drawn as you see it when playing: scroll at the top, G string on the left; open strings sit on the nut. A white zigzag means the neck is cut (higher positions).
Tap a note (on the staff or the fingerboard) to see its name, string, finger and position.
